# Contributing

Revision is open. Anyone may propose a change to the text.

1. Open a pull request against FOUNDATIONAL.md. Say what you are changing and why. If the change comes from something that happened at a table, say that.
2. Discussion happens on the pull request, in the open. Nothing is decided in private.
3. Changes are adopted by the people who keep the practice. Until there are four tables keeping the weekly gathering, the founder merges; after that, custody rotates as the governance section describes.
4. Every adopted change gets a line in CHANGELOG.md and, when it warrants one, a new version tag.

Two limits. The dissolution clause may be made stricter and may never be softened, qualified, or removed; a pull request that weakens it will be closed without discussion. And the card must stay one page.

Forks are welcome. If you fork, keep the dissolution clause and say what you changed.
